One-time fix · 1 minute

Get the right home-screen icon

Your screenshots show the trap. The Add to Home Screen sheet said “Muse”, with the blue Muse icon and the address muse.ai/?__pwa=1. That's Muse's own site, not this page — adding it is exactly why the icon kept asking for a sign-in. You weren't doing the steps wrong; the sheet was pointed at the wrong page.

Before you tap Add, check the sheet. It has to show all three:

  • Icon: the clipboard with a blue dumbbell — not the blue Muse “M”
  • Name: Rahul's Training — not “Muse”
  • Address: starts with muse.ai/s/rahul-s-training — not muse.ai/?__pwa=1

If any of the three is wrong, tap the ✕ to cancel. Don't tap Add.

  1. Delete the wrong icon: long-press the Muse-logo shortcut on your home screen and remove it.
  2. Come back to this workout page — the one showing today's workout, opened from the public link in our chat. Not the Muse home page, and not inside the Muse app.
  3. From this page, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Leave Open as Web App switched on — you had that right.
  4. Check the three things above, then tap Add. The new icon opens full-screen, straight to today's workout — no sign-in, ever.

Every day · Not just run days

Daily lower-back routine — 5 minutes, every day

Do this every day, run day or not. On run days, do it before the run; on rest days, fit it into your morning — daily regardless.

  1. Cat-cow

    Slow · 10 reps

    On hands and knees, arch your back up like a cat, then sag down like a cow, moving with your breath.

    Watch the exact video

  2. Child's pose

    30 seconds

    Knees wide, sit back on your heels, arms stretched forward, forehead to the floor, breathe.

    Watch the exact video

  3. The arc hold (my stretch)

    30 seconds · gentle

    Knees spread like a triangle on the floor, hands on the ground, lift your back up like an arc and hold — then rotate your back gently to both sides, the way the video shows it. Hold each part as shown. Don't force the range, breathe, stop if it's sharp.

    "Stretch to relieve your back pain" — Balanced Motion Clinic. This is the stretch that used to help your right-side lower back.

    Watch the exact video

  4. Bird-dog

    8 each side · slow

    On hands and knees, reach the opposite arm and leg out, keep your hips level and your back flat.

    Watch the exact video

  5. Plank

    After the run · 30-sec holds, 2–3 sets

    After the run, not before. Straight line from head to heels, elbows under shoulders, don't let your hips sag.

    Watch the exact video

Stop if the pain is sharp, and if your back ever feels like it will collapse when you lift Kaia, call your doctor — stretching helps stiffness, it doesn't fix that feeling. When picking Kaia up: bend your knees, keep her close, don't twist.
